Your life was supposed to be settled but when an unexpected letter arrives from Lover Boy (your old summer fling) the past crashes into your present. Your marriage is over, your bags are packed, and you' re driving toward the
dusty, neon lit ranch town you swore you lett behind forever.

This isn't a simple homecoming.

The dusty saloons, cheap motels, and cowboys who feel like figments of memory all blur together, as if the town itself is a fever dream you're coloring in as you go.

"Range Rider" follows a fictional narrator unraveling through desire, nostalgia, fantasy, and regret told through short scenes, prompts, and activities that invite the reader to linger inside the spiral.
